OCR Service Timeout Runbook

Error Code: OCR_TIMEOUT

When the OCR service times out, first verify that the OCR service is reachable and review recent automation logs.

A retry may be attempted if the OCR service is available and the retry count is below 2.

If two retry attempts fail, the incident must be escalated for manual investigation.

OCR recovery does not require human approval when the retry policy conditions are satisfied.
